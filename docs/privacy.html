The short version. Rewind Bowl '87 is an offline single-player football game. It does not collect, transmit or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no server.

WHAT THE APP STORES

The game keeps one save on your device, in the app's local storage. It holds your franchise: club name and colours, season and week, record, tape, roster, settings and history. It never leaves the device.

The app does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or any account sign-in.

WHAT THE APP SENDS

Nothing. The game is a static bundle. It has no server, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK, no attribution SDK and no third-party network requests. It works in airplane mode.

SHARING

The developer does not receive your save or any data from it, so there is nothing to share with third parties. Google Play may collect install and crash information under Google's own policies; that is handled by the store, not by this app.

CHILDREN

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

DELETING YOUR DATA

The save lives only on your device. You can erase it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling removes the career permanently; there is no cloud backup.

CHANGES

If a future version adds a feature that changes any of this — for example cloud saves or analytics — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
