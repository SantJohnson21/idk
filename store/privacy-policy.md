# Privacy policy — Rewind Bowl '87

**Effective date:** 29 October 2026

**App:** Rewind Bowl '87 (`com.rewindbowl.game`)

**Developer contact:** tronjohnson57@gmail.com

**Hosted page (after Pages deploy):** https://santjohnson21.github.io/idk/privacy.html

Rewind Bowl '87 is an offline single-player football game. This policy explains
what the app does with information. The short version: it does not collect,
transmit or share any personal data.

## What the app stores

The game keeps one save on your device, in the app's local storage. That save
contains your franchise: club name and colours, season and week, record, tape,
roster, settings and history. It never leaves the device.

The app does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, location, contacts,
photos, microphone, camera or any account sign-in.

## What the app sends

Nothing. The game is a static bundle. It has no server, no analytics, no crash
reporting, no advertising SDK, no attribution SDK and no third-party network
requests. It works in airplane mode.

## Sharing

The developer does not receive your save or any data from it, so there is nothing
to share with third parties. Google Play may collect install and crash
information under Google's own policies; that is handled by the store, not by
this app.

## Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect
personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

The save lives only on your device. You can erase it by clearing the app's storage
in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling removes the career
permanently; there is no cloud backup.

## Changes

If a future version adds a feature that changes any of this — for example cloud
saves or analytics — this policy will be updated before that version is released,
and the effective date above will change.
